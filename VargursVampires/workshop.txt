title=Vargur's Vampires
description=Vargur's Vampires lets you play as a vampire in Project Zomboid while retaining the game's core systems. This overhaul changes how you approach survival without adding custom meters or disabling mechanics.
description=
description=I built this because I enjoy vampire games and felt other options removed too much of Zomboid's gameplay loop. The goal is mechanically interesting survival, not a power fantasy. With preparation, you can go out in the sun, a bit like [url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oKH93G-2GnQ]Striga in her Day Armour[/url].
description=
description=This mod should interact properly with standard traits that change hunger, thirst, healing, and related systems.
description=
description=[h1]Feature Overview[/h1]
description=[list][*]Start as a vampire by selecting a trait in character creation.[*]Gain +1 Strength, +2 Fitness, +1 Nimble, 80% reduced corpse sickness, and improved health regeneration.[*]Configurable sun damage is applied per body part, with incoming UV modelled on a realistic curve.[*]Reduce sun damage through approximated shade from buildings, eaves, and tree cover.[*]Layer thick clothing to withstand harsh days.[*]Non-meat foods poison vampires; you can eat raw meat, meat, animal blood, and zombie blood.[*]Extract zombie blood from fresh corpses and boil it to make it less poisonous.[*]Add blood as a seasoning to mixed recipes.[*]Adjust difficulty and many other settings with sandbox options.[*]Multiplayer support.[/list]
description=
description=The mod is currently in beta. I have tested it locally and on self-hosted servers; please report bugs. Changes should be localised to vampire players in multiplayer.
description=
description=[h1]Vampire Diet[/h1]
description=Diet changes are configured in code and do not alter vanilla or other modded items. Calorie information therefore will not change in the UI when using Nutritionist, but these effects only apply to players with the Vampire trait. Effects scale with the hunger value on food and ingredients. For example, -10 hunger corresponds to +8 poison.
description=
description=Vampires cannot eat vegetables or other non-meat foods: these foods cause poisoning and unhappiness and provide no calories. Vampires can eat meat and raw meat; raw meat also provides additional nutrition. These changes apply and scale correctly in mixed dishes.
description=
description=You still need to drink normally, and water and other drinks are safe. I did not want to remove thirst as a Zomboid mechanic. Meat is an important part of the diet because vanilla Zomboid otherwise gates blood behind Carpentry 3 and hanging a cow or pig on a hook.
description=
description=[i]Currently supported meat types: Bacon, Beef, Game, Meat, Poultry, Sausage, and Venison.[/i]
description=
description=[h2]Blood[/h2]
description=You can survive entirely on blood. It provides calories, macros, hunger and thirst reduction, slight healing, endurance recovery, improved mood, and mild intoxication.
description=
description=Animal blood is obtained using the vanilla butchering hook method. Human blood is more nutritious and can rarely be found in some medical loot; loot is adjustable in sandbox settings.
description=
description=Zombie blood can be extracted from a corpse with a knife and container. It provides nutrition but is very poisonous. Boiling it improves its nutrition while reducing the quantity, so it is best kept for emergencies.
description=
description=[h1]Sunlight Damage and Protection[/h1]
description=All settings for adjusting UV, damage, and the graph are available in sandbox options.
description=[list][*]UV light changes throughout the day, modelled as an adjusted sine curve.[*]A second curve increases sun damage beyond its peak to 1.4x around noon.[*]Protection factors reduce UV and are applied per body region.[*]After reductions, damage above a clamp is multiplied by base damage weighted for each body region.[*]Entirely uncovered regions have a configurable random chance to receive a burn each tick.[*]Pain is applied according to the rate of incoming damage.[/list]
description=
description=You can go outside during the day if you wear enough thick layers and UV is not too intense. Default settings aim for a relatively normal game with an extra challenge. For a harsher experience, increase base damage above 30 and raise the incoming UV multiplier.
description=
description=In sunlight, you will hear sound and see an overlay. Sunglasses reduce the visual feedback.
description=
description=[i]You can still take reduced sun damage indoors within 2 tiles of a window or other opening. Cover the windows![/i]
description=
description=[h2]Protection Factors[/h2]
description=All attenuation values are adjustable in settings. No single factor should be expected to provide complete daytime protection.
description=[list][*][b]Rain/Precipitation:[/b] Significantly reduces UV, scaling with rainfall.[*][b]Fog:[/b] Less effective than rain, but still helpful.[*][b]Cloud Cover:[/b] Has a small effect, adding some daily randomness.[*][b]Shade:[/b] Trees provide shade, making forests useful for travel. Buildings and small eaves overhead also reduce incoming light.[*][b]Vehicles:[/b] Any vehicle substantially reduces UV, so vehicle travel is not tedious.[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Clothing[/h2]
description=Clothing is your biggest source of daytime protection. Aim for 2+ layers wherever possible and look for items with high wind resistance. Wind resistance approximates clothing thickness and provides a bonus beyond other clothing types.
description=
description=Armour and underlayers count toward sun protection. Wide-brimmed hats, shemaghs, and similar items have special adjustments. Other adjustments help protect areas with fewer clothing options, so you are not always burning your hands or groin. Holes in clothing nullify that layer's protection.
description=
description=[i]Open umbrellas also help a lot![/i]
description=
description=[h1]Traits[/h1]
description=Picking the Vampire trait gives you:
description=[list][*]+1 Strength[*]+2 Fitness[*]+1 Nimble[*]80% reduced corpse sickness[*]3x configurable health regeneration[*]No health regeneration while in the sun[*]Sun damage and dietary changes described above[*]5 points returned to you, as this can be a difficult challenge[/list]
description=
description=A second trait, Sun Resistant, reduces incoming UV by 20%. These traits are intentionally kept relatively vanilla so you can build your character however you like. The goal is to let you play as a vampire without massively disrupting Zomboid's gameplay systems.
description=
description=[h1]Uninstalling Vargur's Vampires[/h1]
description=It is safe to remove this mod from an ongoing save if you first use the included Uninstall sandbox option to clear the mod's features from your save. Set the sandbox variable, then restart the server or reload. The mod will attempt to clear traits and modded blood fluids, and log the results to the console. Afterward, close the server and remove the mod. This option is included because a missing trait can break a character save after mod removal.
